SnapAsk

Select anything on screen. Get an answer. Nothing else.

One-minute setup
  1. Pick a provider and paste an API key below.
  2. Press Alt+Shift+A on any page, or click the SnapAsk icon.
  3. Drag over the thing you are unsure about. That's it.

Provider Your key stays in this browser and is only sent to the provider you choose.
